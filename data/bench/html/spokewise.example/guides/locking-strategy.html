How Bikes Actually Get Stolen

We collected 412 theft reports from readers over two years and looked at where each bike was, how it was locked, and what happened to the lock.

What was beaten

Cable locks were involved in 46 percent of thefts, and in 88 percent of those cases the cable was simply cut. Only 9 percent of stolen bikes were locked with a U-lock of 14 mm or more, and most of those were locked through the front wheel only. Twelve percent were not locked at all, often in a shared garage.

Where

Forty one percent were taken from home: garages, sheds, bike rooms and balconies. Train stations and campus racks were the most common public spots.

Getting it back

Only 11 percent of bikes were recovered. Owners who had a registered serial number and a police report were three times more likely to get their bike back.

What to do

Use a U-lock of at least 14 mm through the frame. Budget $70 to $100 for a lock that stops bolt cutters. Lock the bike at home too.

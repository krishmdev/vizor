Bike Frame Size by Height, Tested on 40 Riders

We put 40 volunteers from 152 to 196 cm tall on three sizes of the same commuter bike and had each ride 10 km on each size, then rate comfort and note any pain.

How often the chart was right

For 29 of the 40 riders, the size the manufacturer chart picked by height was also the size they rated most comfortable. For the other 11, the favorite was one size smaller in 8 cases and one size larger in 3.

Riders between sizes

Riders within 2 cm of a size boundary chose the smaller frame 70 percent of the time. Those with long legs and a short torso preferred the smaller size almost every time.

Reach and back pain

Eleven riders reported lower back or neck pain on at least one size. In 9 of those cases it was on the size with the longest reach. Swapping to a stem 20 mm shorter fixed the pain for 6 of them.

Our advice

Use a chart for a starting point, then test ride. If you are between sizes, choose the smaller one.

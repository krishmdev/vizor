Floor Pumps Tested

We filled a 700 x 28 road tire to 80 psi and a 700 x 42 commuter tire to 50 psi with six floor pumps, and checked each gauge against a calibrated digital gauge.

Best overall: Larkspur Floor Pump

The Larkspur Floor Pump ($49) took 19 strokes to reach 80 psi on the road tire and 24 strokes to 50 psi on the commuter tire. Its gauge read 3 psi high at 80 psi, which is good for a pump under $100. The dual head stayed on Presta valves on every try once pushed fully on.

Fastest: Marro Track HV

The Marro Track HV ($69) needed only 15 strokes on the commuter tire but topped out at 90 psi and its gauge read 7 psi low.

Mini pumps for comparison

A typical 20 cm mini pump took 290 strokes to reach 80 psi on the road tire. Keep one on the bike for emergencies, not for weekly top-ups.

Head problems

In every pump that leaked or popped off, the cause was a head not pushed past the valve threads or a Presta tip left closed.

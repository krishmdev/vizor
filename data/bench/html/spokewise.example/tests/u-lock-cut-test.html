We Tried to Cut Through 7 Bike Locks

We attacked seven locks with a 24 inch bolt cutter, a hacksaw and a battery angle grinder with a new cut-off disc, the tools most often found at theft scenes.

U-locks

The Larkspur Lock D9 ($79, 1.52 kg, 16 mm shackle) beat the bolt cutter and needed two grinder cuts, 1 minute 4 seconds in total. A 13 mm U-lock from a budget brand was cut with the bolt cutter in 9 seconds. The toughest lock we tested, an 18 mm U-lock at $140, took 2 minutes 40 seconds with the grinder but weighed 2.3 kg.

Chain locks

A 10 mm chain with a padlock ($95, 3.4 kg) survived the bolt cutter but its padlock was the weak point and failed in 31 seconds with the grinder. Thin cable locks were cut in under 5 seconds with hand tools.

What this means

No portable lock stops a grinder for long. A good U-lock stops the quick bolt cutter thefts that make up most cases, and at the same strength it weighs roughly half as much as a chain.

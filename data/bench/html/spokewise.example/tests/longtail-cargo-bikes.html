Electric Cargo Bikes Tested With a 60 kg Load

We tested two longtails and one front box bike with 60 kg of sandbags on board, the weight of two primary school children, plus a 75 kg rider.

Larkspur Haul

The Larkspur Haul ($3,899) went 52 km on one charge with the full load and climbed our 400 m, 10 percent hill in 2 minutes 5 seconds at the top assist level. It weighed 34.6 kg on our scale. It handled most like a normal bike of the three.

Mulehaus Long

The Mulehaus Long ($4,400) had a bigger 800 Wh battery and went 61 km, but its 60 Nm motor slowed to walking pace on the hill when we started in too high a gear.

Brisa Kargo (front box)

The front box Brisa Kargo ($2,900) was the cheapest and fit the most cargo, but at 41 kg and 68 cm wide it was hard to steer through bike path barriers.

Climbing with a heavy load

Every bike climbed better when we downshifted before the hill. Starting a climb in a high gear cut speed by up to a third and made the motors run hot.

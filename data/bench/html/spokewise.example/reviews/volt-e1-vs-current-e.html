Larkspur Volt E1 vs Cog & Chain Current E, Tested

We rode both commuter e-bikes on the same 22 km loop with 310 m of climbing, same 78 kg rider, middle assist level, until each battery cut out.

Range

The Cog & Chain Current E went 71 km. The Larkspur Volt E1 went 58 km. The Current E has a slightly bigger battery (540 Wh against 504 Wh), but most of the gap came from the mid-drive using the gears on climbs. Per watt-hour, the Current E averaged 7.6 Wh per km and the Volt E1 8.7 Wh per km.

Hills

On our 9 percent test climb the Current E was 14 seconds faster over 600 m. The Volt E1's hub motor got noticeably warm on repeated climbs but never cut power.

Weight and noise

On our scale the Volt E1 weighed 23.3 kg and the Current E 22.0 kg. The Volt E1 was quieter, at 49 dB against 55 dB at 25 km/h.

Verdict

The Current E ($2,499) is the better bike for hilly commutes. The Volt E1 ($2,199) costs $300 less, is quieter and will wear chains more slowly, which makes it the better value on flat routes.

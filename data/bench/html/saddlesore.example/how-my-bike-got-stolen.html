How My Bike Got Stolen

Two years ago I locked my commuter outside the train station with a cable lock and came back 40 minutes later to a cut cable on the ground. The man at the coffee kiosk said it took the thief less than a minute.

The first 24 hours

I filed a police report online with my serial number, which I luckily had in a photo on my phone. I posted the bike on two stolen bike registries and our local cycling group. I checked marketplace listings every day for three weeks.

Did I get it back

No. The police said most bikes are gone within hours. My home insurance paid out after about six weeks, minus a $250 excess.

How I lock now

I bought a proper U-lock for $85, lock through the frame and the back wheel to a fixed rack, and use a small cable for the front wheel. I lock the bike even in our building's bike room. So far so good.

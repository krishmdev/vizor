How I Picked My Frame Size

My first commuter was a large, because the shop had it on sale and I'm 180 cm. After a month my lower back ached every morning and my hands went numb.

What was wrong

I have short arms and a long torso for my height, so the large put the bars too far away. I was reaching with locked elbows. A shorter stem helped, but I still felt stretched.

The second time

I measured my inseam at 82 cm and test rode a medium and a large of the same bike back to back, for 20 minutes each. The medium let me bend my elbows and sit a bit more upright. I bought the medium, and my back pain went away within two weeks.

My advice

Don't buy from a chart alone. If you're between sizes, try both, and pay attention to your hands and back, not just your legs.

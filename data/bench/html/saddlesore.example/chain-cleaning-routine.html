My Chain Cleaning Routine

I ruined a cassette and chainrings in my second winter because I never cleaned my chain. A $180 repair taught me to spend ten minutes every other weekend on it.

What I do

Rag wipe first. Then an old toothbrush and a splash of degreaser on the worst bits, rinse with a watering can, dry with a towel. One drop of lube per link, spin, wait five minutes, wipe the outside hard. The outside of a chain should look almost dry.

Wet vs dry lube

I tried dry lube in winter and the chain was squeaking by Tuesday. Now I use wet lube from October to April and dry lube in summer. When it squeaks after a rainy ride I just relube it that evening.

How often

My commute is 9 km each way, so roughly every 180 km. I also check it with a $10 wear gauge each month.

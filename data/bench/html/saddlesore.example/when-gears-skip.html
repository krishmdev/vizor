When Gears Skip: What Worked for Me

Last spring my commuter started jumping gears every time I stood up on the pedals on the hill near my house. It was scary.

First try: the barrel adjuster

I watched a video and turned the barrel adjuster by the derailleur. A quarter turn at a time, shifting after each one. It fixed the clicking in the middle gears, but the jumping under hard pedaling was still there.

The real problem

I borrowed a chain checker from a friend. My chain was well past 0.75 percent. I had done around 5,000 km on it. New chain plus a new cassette cost me $70 in parts, and the skipping was gone that day.

What I tell people now

If it skips only when you push hard, check the chain wear before anything else. If it skips or rattles all the time, it's probably cable tension, and a shop will sort that for about $30 if you don't want to do it yourself.

What Tire Pressure I Run on My Commuter

For years I pumped my tires to the maximum number on the sidewall, 85 psi, because I thought hard meant fast. My hands went numb on every ride.

What I run now

My commuter has 35 mm tires and I weigh 82 kg. I run 48 psi front and 55 rear. With a full pannier on the back I add 5 psi to the rear. The bike feels calmer over potholes and I honestly don't notice it being any slower. My average speed on the same 11 km route barely moved.

How I found it

I started at the maximum and dropped 5 psi each week until the tire felt soft in corners, then went back up one step. Too low, and you feel it squirm and risk a pinch flat.

My routine

Every Sunday night I check both tires with the floor pump gauge. They lose about 6 psi a week.

My First Month of Bike Commuting

I started riding the 8 km to my office last April. I was nervous about traffic and about showing up gross. Both turned out to be fixable.

The route

My first ride followed the car route and it was terrifying. The second week I found a route through a park and two quiet streets. It's 1 km longer and ten times nicer.

The sweat problem

I was riding way too hard, like it was a race. Now I ride at a pace where I could hold a conversation, and I coast the last few hundred metres. I keep a towel and deodorant in my desk and bring a fresh shirt in my pannier. That's it.

What I bought

A helmet, lights, a U-lock and a pannier came to about $260. I didn't need special clothes for the first month.

I started with two days a week. By the end of the month I was riding four.

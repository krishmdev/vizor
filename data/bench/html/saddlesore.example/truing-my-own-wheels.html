Truing My Own Wheels

I hit a pothole last autumn and my back wheel started rubbing the brake pad with every turn. The shop was booked for a week, so I tried to fix it myself.

What I did

I flipped the bike and zip-tied a cable tie to the frame so it just touched the rim at the wobble. Then quarter turns on the spoke key: tighten the spokes on the side away from the rub, loosen the ones on the rub side. Spin, check, repeat. It took me 40 minutes the first time and about ten now.

My mistake

I once turned a nipple the wrong way because the spoke key directions are backwards when you look from the tire side. Take it slow and check after each turn.

When I go to the shop

A broken spoke or a dented rim. My local shop charges $30 for a true, which is worth it for anything big.

How I Finally Fixed My Squeaky Disc Brakes

My disc brakes were so loud in the rain that people on the sidewalk would turn around. It took me a while, but they are quiet now.

The real cause

I was spraying chain lube with the wheel in the bike, and some of it drifted onto the rear rotor. That meant squealing even on dry days. I cleaned both rotors with isopropyl alcohol and a clean paper towel and threw away the pads, since oily pads never really come back.

Bedding in

With the new pads, I did twenty hard slow-downs from jogging speed on a quiet street, without stopping fully. The difference was huge, both for noise and power.

Rain noise

A bit of squeal on the first couple of stops in the rain still happens, and I've made peace with it. It goes away after a few stops. I also switched to sintered pads, which are a bit louder but last way longer in the wet.

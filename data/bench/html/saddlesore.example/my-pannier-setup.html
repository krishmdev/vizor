My Pannier Setup After Three Years of Commuting

I rode with a backpack for my first year and got to work with a soaked back every single day, even in winter. Switching to a pannier fixed that.

What I use

I use one Larkspur Pannier 20, which cost me $95. It swallows my 14 inch laptop, a shirt, a lunch box and a lock. It has survived three winters of rain without a drop getting in, as long as I roll the top down properly.

The rattle problem

For the first month the bag bounced off twice on a pothole. The hooks were too wide for my thin rack tubes. I fitted the little plastic inserts that came with it and moved the lower hook down so it pulls the bag tight. It has not moved since.

Backpack or pannier

If your ride is under 3 km, a backpack is fine. Anything longer and the pannier wins. The only real downside is carrying it around the office, so I bought one with a shoulder strap.

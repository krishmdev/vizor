Knee Pain and Saddle Height

For most of my first year riding, the front of both knees ached after every commute. I assumed it was just part of cycling. It wasn't.

The cause

My saddle was set low because I liked being able to touch the ground from the seat. A friend watched me ride and pointed out my knees were still very bent at the bottom of every pedal stroke.

The fix

I used the heel method: heel on the pedal, leg straight at the bottom. That meant raising my saddle 25 mm. I did it in three steps over a week because it felt strange. The ache was gone in about ten days.

A warning

I overdid it once and went 10 mm too high, and the back of my knee started hurting instead. Small steps. And you're not supposed to reach the ground from the saddle; you slide forward off it when you stop.

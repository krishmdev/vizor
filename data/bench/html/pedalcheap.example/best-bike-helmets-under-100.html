Best Bike Helmets Under $100

Your helmet is the most important piece of cycling gear you will buy. Luckily, good helmets don't have to be expensive.

Our picks

The Cog & Chain Dome costs $79 and has a rotational liner. The Larkspur Shell is $89 and even has a light built into the back, which is a great bonus for commuters. Both look good and are comfortable.

All helmets sold in the US have to pass safety standards, so even cheap helmets protect you.

Tips

Make sure it fits snugly. Pick a bright color so drivers can see you. Replace your helmet after a crash.

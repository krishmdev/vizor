6 Best Bike Locks That Thieves Hate

Bike theft is a huge problem in cities, so a good lock is a must. Here are our top picks.

Best U-locks

The Larkspur Lock D9 is a solid U-lock for $79 and comes with a frame mount. U-locks are the most popular choice for city commuters because they are strong and easy to carry.

Chain locks are also great because they can wrap around almost anything, but they can be heavy.

Tips

Spend about 10 percent of what your bike cost on a lock. Never use a cable lock alone. Always lock your bike to something solid.

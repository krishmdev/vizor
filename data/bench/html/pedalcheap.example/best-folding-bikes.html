The 7 Best Folding Bikes for Commuters

Folding bikes are super handy if you take the train or live in a small apartment. We rounded up the best folding bikes you can buy.

Best picks

The Cog & Chain Pocket 20 is one of the smallest folding bikes out there and costs $899. The Larkspur Fold 20 is a little cheaper at $749 and has more gears. Both are amazing choices for city commuters.

Folding bikes with small wheels are fun to ride and easy to carry. Most fold in less than a minute.

Buying tips

Check the folded size and the weight before you buy, especially if you have to carry it up stairs. Cheaper folding bikes can be heavy. Don't forget a lock!

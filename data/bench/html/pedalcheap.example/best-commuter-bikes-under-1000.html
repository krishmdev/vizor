9 Best Commuter Bikes Under $1000 You Can Buy Right Now (2026)

Biking to work is one of the best ways to save money and stay fit. Here are our favorite commuter bikes under $1000.

Our top picks

The Cog & Chain Transit 8 is a great all-rounder at $799 and comes with fenders and a rack. The Larkspur Metro 7 costs $849 and has an internal gear hub, which is awesome if you hate maintenance. Both are some of the best city bikes around.

If you want something cheaper, big box store bikes can work for short rides, but they are usually heavier.

What to look for

Look for fenders, a rack, and good brakes. Disc brakes are nice to have. Try to get a bike that is not too heavy if you have stairs. Always check the latest prices, because deals come and go fast.

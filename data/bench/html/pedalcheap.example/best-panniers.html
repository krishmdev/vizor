Best Panniers for Commuting

Panniers are bags that clip onto your bike rack. They are a game changer for commuting because you don't have to wear a sweaty backpack.

Our favorites

The Larkspur Pannier 20 is waterproof and has a laptop sleeve, and it costs $95. Roll-top panniers are the most waterproof type. Some cheaper panniers come in pairs, so shop around.

Most commuters only need one pannier, but two is great for grocery runs. Just remember you need a rear rack first.

Things to check

Make sure the bag fits your rack. Look for reflective details. A shoulder strap makes it easier to carry off the bike.

Larkspur Haul

The Larkspur Haul is an electric longtail cargo bike. It is built to replace a second car for school runs, grocery shopping and commuting with a lot to carry.

Why a longtail

A longtail carries its load on an extended rear rack behind the rider. Compared with a front box cargo bike, a longtail is narrower, rides and steers much like a normal bike, and fits through standard doors and bike paths. A front box bike keeps children in view in front of you and fits a bigger load, but it is wider, heavier and takes some practice to steer. For two children and a bag, a longtail is usually the easier bike to live with.

The rear deck takes up to two child seats, or seat pads with a wraparound handrail and footboards for older children. Wheel guards keep feet out of the spokes.

Motor and gears

The Haul uses a mid-drive motor, which works through the gears. On a steep hill with a full load, shift down early to a low gear and use the highest assist level. A high cadence keeps the motor in its strongest range and stops it from overheating.

Handling and stopping

A dual-leg center stand holds the bike level while children climb on. Four-piston hydraulic disc brakes are sized for heavy loads.

Specifications

The motor is 250 W nominal with 85 Nm of torque. The battery is 720 Wh, with a rated range of 45 to 80 km. The rear rack carries 80 kg, and the maximum total weight including rider is 200 kg. The drivetrain is 10-speed and the wheels are 20 inch. The bike weighs 34 kg.

The Larkspur Haul costs $3,899 including the rear handrail and wheel guards.

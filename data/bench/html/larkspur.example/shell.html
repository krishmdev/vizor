Larkspur Shell

The Larkspur Shell is a commuter helmet for daily riding in the city. It has a built-in rear light, a small visor and a fit system that works with a hat underneath in winter.

Protection

Every Shell meets the CPSC and EN 1078 bicycle helmet standards. Inside the foam there is a thin low-friction liner we call SlipLayer. In an angled impact it lets the head move a few millimetres relative to the helmet, which is designed to reduce the rotational forces that are linked to concussion. A helmet without such a liner still protects well against direct impacts, which is what the standards test, but the liner adds protection for the angled falls that are common on a bike.

Fit

A helmet only protects you if it fits. Measure around your head about 2 cm above your eyebrows with a soft tape, and choose the size that puts you in the middle of its range. Once adjusted, the helmet should sit level, about two fingers above the eyebrows, and should not rock when you shake your head.

A rear dial tightens the fit in small steps, and the straps meet just below the ear.

Light and vents

The rear light is rechargeable and has steady and flashing modes. Eleven vents keep air moving on warm days.

Sizes and specifications

The Shell comes in three sizes: small fits 51 to 55 cm, medium 55 to 59 cm and large 59 to 63 cm. A medium weighs 330 g. The rear light gives 25 lumens and runs 6 hours on flash.

The Larkspur Shell costs $89. Replace it after any crash, or after five years.

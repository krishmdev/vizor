Larkspur Lock D9

The Larkspur Lock D9 is a hardened steel U-lock for commuters who leave their bike on the street every day.

Why a U-lock

A U-lock is the best mix of security, weight and price for most city riders. Thieves usually attack locks with bolt cutters, and a thick hardened shackle is much harder to cut than a cable or a thin chain. A heavy chain lock can match a U-lock for security and wraps around bigger objects, but a chain of the same strength weighs two to three times as much.

The D9 shackle locks into the crossbar at both ends, so a thief has to make two cuts, not one, to remove it.

Size

The inside of the shackle is long enough to go around a frame tube and a bike rack or a thin post. It is too small for most lamp posts and trees, which is on purpose: a snug lock leaves less room for a jack or a lever.

Keys and care

The lock comes with three keys and a key number you can register with us for replacements. If the key gets stiff or hard to turn, especially in freezing weather, spray a dry PTFE lube into the keyhole and work the key a few times. Do not use oil, which collects dirt and can gum up in the cold.

Specifications

The shackle is 16 mm hardened steel. The inside size is 102 x 229 mm. The lock weighs 1.52 kg and comes with a frame mount. In our tests it resisted a 24 inch bolt cutter, and it took 58 seconds to cut through with a battery angle grinder.

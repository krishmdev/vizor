Larkspur Pannier 20

The Larkspur Pannier 20 is a waterproof rear pannier for carrying a laptop, a change of clothes and lunch to work.

Why a pannier

A backpack is simple and goes anywhere, but on a bike it traps heat against your back and raises your centre of gravity. A pannier lets the bike carry the weight low on the rack, so you arrive with a dry back and the bike handles better. The downside is that you need a rack, and a pannier is less comfortable to carry around off the bike.

Waterproof construction

The Pannier 20 is made from a welded, coated fabric with a roll-top closure. Roll the top down at least three times and clip it for a full seal. A padded sleeve inside holds a laptop off the bottom of the bag.

Mounting

The bag hooks onto the top rail of a rear rack and a lower hook clips to the rack's lower strut. If your pannier bounces or jumps off over bumps, the top hooks are probably set too wide for your rack tube. Fit the included reducer inserts for thin tubes, slide the hooks so they sit close to the rack stays, and set the lower hook so it pulls the bag tight against the rack. A bag that swings at the bottom will also rub your heel.

Specifications

The Pannier 20 holds 20 litres and fits a 15 inch laptop. It weighs 1.05 kg. The top hooks fit rack tubes from 8 to 16 mm. A reflective strip runs along the back and a shoulder strap is included.

The Larkspur Pannier 20 costs $95 and is sold singly.

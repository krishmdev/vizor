Larkspur Spin T2

The Larkspur Spin T2 is a smart direct-drive indoor trainer for riding at home through winter or on days when the commute is not an option. It connects to common training and virtual riding apps.

Direct drive

With a direct-drive trainer you remove the rear wheel and mount the bike on the trainer's own cassette. A wheel-on trainer is cheaper and quicker to set up because the bike keeps its wheel, but the tire slips under hard efforts, wears quickly and makes more noise, and the power reading changes with tire pressure. Direct drive is quieter, more accurate and feels closer to riding on the road.

Power accuracy

The Spin T2 measures power with strain gauges and calibrates itself automatically. If its power reading does not match your bike's power meter, first check that both are calibrated. A spin-down or zero offset before the ride, after a 10 minute warm-up, fixes most differences. A gap of a few percent is normal because the trainer measures at the hub, after some power is lost in the chain.

Setup

The T2 fits 130, 135 and 142 mm rear spacing and comes with an 11-speed cassette fitted. The legs fold for storage.

Specifications

The Spin T2 handles up to 1,800 W and simulates climbs up to 18 percent. Claimed power accuracy is plus or minus 2.5 percent. It weighs 16.8 kg and measured 59 dB at 250 W in our tests.

The Larkspur Spin T2 costs $749.

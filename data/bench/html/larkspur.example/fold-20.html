Larkspur Fold 20

The Larkspur Fold 20 is a folding bike for commutes that mix riding with a train, bus or car. It folds down small enough to carry onto a crowded train and slide under a desk at work.

Folding

The Fold 20 folds in three steps. The frame hinges in the middle, the handlebar stem folds down, and the seatpost drops to lock the package together. There are no loose parts and nothing to remove. With a little practice most owners fold it without looking.

Magnets hold the two halves together when folded, and the bike can be rolled on its rear wheel like a suitcase instead of carried.

Riding

The 20 inch wheels make the Fold 20 quick to steer in traffic. The long seatpost and telescoping stem adjust for riders from about 150 to 195 cm. Mechanical disc brakes and an eight-speed derailleur give it the stopping and gearing of a full-size city bike.

Fitting a rack and bags

A front carrier block on the head tube takes a bag that stays in place when the bike is folded. Fender mounts are included.

Specifications

The Fold 20 weighs 11.6 kg. It folds in about 15 seconds, and the folded size is 60 x 80 x 36 cm. The maximum rider weight is 105 kg. Tires are 20 x 1.5 inch.

The Larkspur Fold 20 costs $749 and comes with a five-year frame and hinge warranty.

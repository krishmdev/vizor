Larkspur Metro 7

The Larkspur Metro 7 is our everyday city bike. It is built for riding to work, school and the shops in normal clothes, in any weather, without much fuss.

Designed for city streets

The frame is aluminum with an upright riding position, so you sit high enough to see over parked cars and turn your head easily at junctions. The fenders, rear rack, kickstand and full chainguard come fitted from the factory, which means you can ride the Metro 7 home from the shop and commute on it the next morning.

We chose a seven-speed internal gear hub instead of a derailleur. The gears are sealed inside the rear hub, so road grit and rain do not reach them, and you can change gear while stopped at a red light. A hub needs far less adjustment than a derailleur and is hard to knock out of line when the bike is parked in a crowded rack. The trade-off is a little extra weight and a narrower gear range, which suits flat and rolling cities better than steep hills.

Brakes and tires

Hydraulic disc brakes stop the same way in the wet as in the dry. The tires have a puncture protection belt and a reflective sidewall stripe.

Sizes and fit

The Metro 7 comes in small, medium and large and fits riders from about 155 to 195 cm tall.

Specifications

A medium Metro 7 weighs 13.4 kg with fenders, rack and chainguard fitted. Brakes use 160 mm rotors front and rear. Tires are 700 x 40c. The rear rack is rated for 25 kg.

The Larkspur Metro 7 costs $849 and includes a lifetime frame warranty and a free first service after 300 km.

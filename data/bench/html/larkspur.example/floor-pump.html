Larkspur Floor Pump

The Larkspur Floor Pump is a steel-barrel track pump for keeping your tires at the right pressure at home. Checking pressure once a week is the single easiest way to prevent flats and make your bike roll faster.

Why a floor pump

A mini pump is made to fit in a bag for roadside repairs. It works, but it takes hundreds of short strokes to fill a tire and most have no gauge. A floor pump moves far more air per stroke, stands on the ground so you can use your body weight, and has a gauge so you know the actual pressure. Most riders keep a floor pump at home and a mini pump or CO2 inflator on the bike.

The pump head

The dual head fits both Presta and Schrader valves without swapping parts. For a Presta valve, first unscrew the small tip on the valve and press it once to free it. Push the head straight on, all the way down past the threads, then flip the lever up to lock it. If the head pops off or leaks, it is usually not pushed on far enough, or it has been put on at an angle, which can bend the valve.

Gauge

The gauge sits at the top of the barrel, where you can read it while pumping.

Specifications

The pump reaches 160 psi (11 bar). It stands 68 cm tall, weighs 1.9 kg and has a 90 cm hose. In our tests it took 18 strokes to fill a 700 x 28 tire from flat to 80 psi.

The Larkspur Floor Pump costs $49 and has a two-year warranty. A replacement head seal kit costs $6.

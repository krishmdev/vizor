Larkspur Sprout 16

The Larkspur Sprout 16 is a first pedal bike for young children who have outgrown a balance bike or are ready to ride without one.

Built for small riders

Children are much lighter than adults, so a heavy bike is harder for them to handle than it looks. We kept the Sprout 16 light with an aluminum frame and fork, and gave it a low standover height so a child can put both feet flat on the ground when stopped.

The cranks are short and the handlebar is narrow, matched to the size of a four to six year old. The chain is fully covered to keep fingers and trousers away from it.

Brakes

The Sprout 16 has two short-reach hand brakes and no coaster brake. A coaster brake, which works by pedaling backward, is common on kids bikes, but it makes it hard for children to find their starting pedal position, and it does not teach the hand braking they will need on every bike after this one. Our levers are sized so a small hand can pull them hard.

Training wheels

Removable training wheels are included. Most children who have ridden a balance bike will not need them.

Sizing and specifications

The Sprout 16 fits children from about 100 to 120 cm tall, which is typically ages four to six. A five year old of average height, around 110 cm, is right in the middle of the range. Seat height adjusts from 50 to 62 cm. The bike weighs 7.2 kg.

The Larkspur Sprout 16 costs $329.

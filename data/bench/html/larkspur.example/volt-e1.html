Larkspur Volt E1

The Larkspur Volt E1 is a commuter e-bike for people who want to get to work without arriving tired, and who ride on a mix of city streets, bike paths and short hills.

How the motor works

The Volt E1 uses a rear hub motor. Hub motors are quiet, cheap to maintain and do not put extra wear on the chain and cassette, because the motor pushes the wheel directly. A mid-drive motor, which drives the cranks, climbs steep hills more efficiently and feels more natural, but it costs more and wears drivetrain parts faster. For a mostly flat or rolling commute, a hub motor is the simpler choice.

A cadence sensor turns the motor on when you pedal. There are four assist levels and a walk mode for pushing the bike up ramps.

Everyday details

Integrated front and rear lights run off the main battery. Fenders, a rack and hydraulic disc brakes come fitted. The battery locks into the down tube and slides out so you can charge it indoors.

Range

Range depends on assist level, rider weight, hills, wind and temperature. Expect less range in cold weather and at the highest assist level.

Specifications

The motor is 250 W nominal with 45 Nm of torque, and assist stops at 32 km/h (20 mph). The battery is 504 Wh and charges from empty to full in about 4.5 hours. Rated range is 40 to 90 km. In our own testing on a hilly 18 km commute loop at the second assist level, a 75 kg rider got 62 km from one charge. The bike weighs 23.1 kg including the battery.

The Larkspur Volt E1 costs $2,199 and includes a two-year warranty on the motor and battery.

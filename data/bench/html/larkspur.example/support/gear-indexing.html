Gears Skipping and Indexing

When a derailleur bike skips, jumps between gears or will not shift cleanly, the cause is usually cable tension. This Larkspur support page explains how to fix indexing yourself with the barrel adjuster.

Why gears skip

New shift cables stretch slightly during the first few hundred kilometres, which lets the derailleur drift out of line. Other causes are a bent derailleur hanger after the bike falls over, and a worn chain and cassette. If the chain skips only when you pedal hard, and especially on the smaller cogs you use most, the chain and cassette are probably worn out and adjusting will not help.

Adjusting the barrel adjuster

Shift to the smallest rear cog. Find the barrel adjuster, usually where the cable leaves the shifter or enters the derailleur. Shift up one gear. If the chain is slow to climb, turn the barrel counterclockwise a quarter turn to add tension. If it climbs past the gear or rattles, turn it clockwise. Keep shifting through all gears and adjusting in quarter turns until shifts are clean both ways.

When to go to a shop

If indexing will not stay put, the hanger may be bent, and a shop can straighten it with an alignment tool.

Wear and costs

Check chain wear every 1,000 km. A chain replaced at 0.5 percent stretch will usually let a cassette last through two or three chains. A shop gear adjustment usually costs $20 to $40, and a hanger alignment about $25. Larkspur bikes such as the Gravel GX come with a free first service after 300 km, which includes an indexing check. The Metro 7 uses an internal hub, which rarely needs adjustment.

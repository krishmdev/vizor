Larkspur Gravel GX

The Larkspur Gravel GX is a drop-bar bike for long commutes, weekend rides on dirt roads and loaded day trips. It is built to be fast on pavement and stable on gravel.

Frame and geometry

The Gravel GX has an aluminum frame and a carbon fork. The geometry is a little longer and lower at the front than a hybrid, but more relaxed than a road race bike, so it stays comfortable for an hour or more in the saddle.

Compared with a flat-bar hybrid, the drop bars give you several hand positions and a lower, more efficient position into a headwind. A hybrid is more upright and easier to see traffic from, which some riders prefer for short city trips. For a commute longer than about 15 km with a mix of roads and paths, a gravel bike is usually the faster and more comfortable choice.

Mounts

There are mounts for full fenders, a rear rack and three bottles, so the Gravel GX can be a year-round commuter.

Tires and clearance

Wider tires let you run lower pressure for grip and comfort on loose surfaces. Tire clearance matters because you also need room for mud and fenders.

Specifications

The Gravel GX ships with 700 x 40 mm tubeless-ready tires and has clearance for 700 x 45 mm tires, or 650b x 2.0 inch. With fenders fitted, the maximum is 700 x 40 mm. The drivetrain is 2 x 10 with a 46/30 crankset and an 11-36 cassette. Brakes are hydraulic disc.

A size 54 Gravel GX weighs 9.8 kg without pedals. The Larkspur Gravel GX costs $1,399.

Cog & Chain Pocket 20

The Pocket 20 is our smallest folding bike, made for train commuters and anyone with no room to store a full-size bike.

Why the Pocket 20

The Pocket 20 folds sideways at the frame and the handlebar in one motion, and the rear triangle swings under to lock everything in place. Folded, it measures 58 x 64 x 30 cm, small enough for most train luggage racks and the space under a desk.

Every Pocket 20 comes with a cover bag that turns the folded bike into ordinary luggage, and a front bag mount that stays attached when the bike is folded, so your work bag rides with it.

At 10.8 kg it is light enough to carry up station stairs with one hand. The 6-speed gearing is lower than on a full-size bike, which suits stop-and-go city riding more than long fast roads.

Specifications

20 inch wheels with 1.35 inch tires. 6-speed derailleur. Rim brakes. Fold time about 12 seconds. Folded size 58 x 64 x 30 cm. Weight 10.8 kg. Maximum rider weight 110 kg. Price $899.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take the Pocket 20 on the train at rush hour?

Most rail operators allow folded bikes at all times because they count as luggage. Check your local rules, but at 58 x 64 x 30 cm the Pocket 20 fits the usual limits.

Is a 20 inch folding bike slower than a normal bike?

Slightly. Small wheels accelerate quickly but lose a little speed on rough roads. For commutes under 10 km most riders notice no difference.

How long does it take to learn to fold?

Most owners fold it in under 20 seconds after a day or two.

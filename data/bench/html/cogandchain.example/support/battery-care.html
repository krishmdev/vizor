E-Bike Battery Care

A well treated e-bike battery lasts five years or more. These are the habits our service team recommends for every Cog & Chain Current E owner.

Charging

Charge with the original charger, at room temperature, on a hard surface. Unplug once it is full. If you ride the same short commute every day, stopping at 80 or 90 percent adds years to the battery. Leaving it on the charger at 100 percent for days is the habit that ages batteries fastest.

Winter

Batteries lose range in the cold because the chemistry slows down. Keep the battery inside overnight and put it on the bike right before you ride. A neoprene battery cover helps on rides longer than 30 minutes.

Storage

For storage longer than a few weeks, leave the battery at about half charge in a cool, dry room, and top it up every eight weeks. Never leave the battery in a hot car in summer or on a cold balcony in winter. Below 0 °C, let it warm to room temperature before charging.

Frequently asked questions

Should I charge my e-bike battery to 100 percent?

Only when you need the full range. For daily use, 80 to 90 percent is kinder to the cells and still covers most commutes.

Why is my range lower in winter?

Cold slows the chemical reaction in the cells. Expect 15 to 30 percent less range below 5 °C. It is not permanent damage.

When should I replace my battery?

When it holds less than about 70 percent of its original range. Current E replacement batteries cost $649.

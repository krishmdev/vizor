How to True a Bike Wheel at Home

Small wobbles are easy to fix at home in 15 minutes with a spoke key and patience.

Find the wobble

Spin the wheel in the bike and hold a pencil or a zip tie close to the rim. Mark where it touches. On rim brake bikes the brake pads make a handy guide. Also check for hops, where the rim moves up and down toward the hub. These are harder to fix and usually need a truing stand.

Adjust the spokes

Spokes pull the rim toward the side of the hub they attach to. To move the rim away from the rub, tighten the spoke going to the opposite side of the hub at the center of the wobble by half a turn, and loosen the spokes on either side of it by a quarter turn. Work slowly; big turns create new problems.

When to use a shop

If a spoke is broken, the rim is dented or the wheel will not come straight, bring it in. Truing at a Cog & Chain store costs $25, or $35 with a spoke replacement.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my back wheel rub the brake?

Either the wheel is out of true, or it is not sitting straight in the frame. Loosen the axle, center the wheel and tighten again before truing.

Can I ride on a wobbly wheel?

A small wobble is safe to ride home on. A large wobble or a broken spoke can damage the rim, so ride gently and get it fixed.

What tools do I need?

A spoke key that fits your spoke nipples. A truing stand makes it easier, but you can true the wheel in the bike.

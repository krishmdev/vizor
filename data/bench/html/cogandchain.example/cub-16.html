Cog & Chain Cub 16

The Cub 16 is a light first pedal bike for children aged about 4 to 6.

Why the Cub 16

At 6.3 kg the Cub 16 is one of the lightest 16 inch bikes you can buy, which makes it easier for a small child to start, balance and pick up after a fall.

The seat clamp is a tool-free quick release, so the saddle can go up as your child grows. Most children get two to three seasons out of a Cub 16 before moving to a 20 inch bike.

The Cub 16 pairs a rear coaster brake with a front hand brake. Many children already understand pedaling backward to stop, and the hand brake lets them learn lever braking at the same time. Parents can remove the coaster brake with a conversion kit when their child is confident with the lever.

Specifications

Aluminum frame, steel fork. 16 x 1.95 inch tires. Rear coaster brake and front V-brake. Seat height 49 to 61 cm. Fits riders 98 to 117 cm tall. Weight 6.3 kg. Price $279.

Frequently asked questions

What size bike does a 5 year old need?

Most five year olds ride a 16 inch bike. Measure your child's inseam; they should be able to touch the ground with both feet on the saddle at its lowest setting.

Are coaster brakes bad for kids?

Not bad, but they make it harder to learn hand braking. That is why the Cub 16 also has a front hand brake.

Does the Cub 16 come with training wheels?

No. We recommend starting on a balance bike instead, but standard training wheels fit.

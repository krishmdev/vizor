Cog & Chain Current E

The Current E is our commuter e-bike for riders with real hills on the way to work.

Why a mid-drive

The Current E puts its motor at the cranks. Because the motor drives through the bike's gears, it can stay in its efficient speed range on a steep climb instead of bogging down. A torque sensor measures how hard you push and adds power in proportion, so the bike feels like a strong version of a normal bicycle rather than a scooter.

The battery sits inside the down tube and comes out with a key. The display estimates remaining range from your last 10 km of riding, which is more useful on a hilly commute than a simple five-bar gauge.

The trade-off is that a mid-drive puts more load on the chain and cassette. Expect to replace a chain every 1,500 to 2,500 km.

Specifications

250 W mid-drive motor, 65 Nm. 540 Wh removable battery, 5 hour charge. Rated range up to 100 km in eco mode. Assist to 32 km/h. 10-speed drivetrain, hydraulic disc brakes, fenders, rack and lights. Weight 21.9 kg. Price $2,499 with a two-year warranty.

Frequently asked questions

How far can the Current E go on one charge?

Up to 100 km in eco mode on flat roads. Most commuters using the middle assist level see 55 to 75 km.

Is a mid-drive worth the extra cost?

If your commute has steep hills, yes. On flat routes a hub motor e-bike does the job for less money.

Can I charge the battery at work?

Yes. The battery slides out with the key and uses a compact 4 A charger that fits in a pannier.

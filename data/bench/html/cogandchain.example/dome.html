Cog & Chain Dome

The Dome is a simple, comfortable helmet for everyday riding. It is built for commuters who wear a helmet every day and want one that is light, easy to buckle and does not cost much.

Why the Dome

The Dome has a rotational liner under the pads, the same idea used in more expensive helmets. Standard helmets are tested with straight drops onto an anvil, but real falls usually hit at an angle. A rotational liner lets the shell slide slightly on impact so less twisting force reaches the brain.

A magnetic buckle clips shut with one hand, even in gloves. The dial at the back adjusts the fit, and the rear cradle moves up and down to suit different hairstyles. The Dome has no built-in light, so pair it with a clip-on light if you ride at night.

Specifications

Sizes S (52 to 56 cm), M (56 to 59 cm) and L (59 to 62 cm). Weight 350 g (M). 14 vents. Certified to CPSC and EN 1078. Price $79.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my helmet size?

Wrap a soft tape measure around your head just above the eyebrows and ears. If you fall between two sizes, try both; the smaller size with the dial loosened usually fits better.

Is a rotational liner worth it?

We think so. It adds only a few grams and a few dollars, and it is designed to lower rotational forces in angled impacts.

When should I replace my helmet?

After any crash where your head hit the ground, even with no visible damage, and otherwise every five years.

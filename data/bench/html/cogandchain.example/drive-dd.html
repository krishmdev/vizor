Cog & Chain Drive DD

The Drive DD is our direct drive smart trainer for structured training and virtual rides through the winter.

Why the Drive DD

The Drive DD replaces your rear wheel, so there is no tire to slip or wear out. Power is measured at the flywheel axle and is accurate to within 1.5 percent, close enough to match most pedal and crank power meters.

A heavy flywheel gives a smooth feel on flat roads, and the brake can simulate climbs up to 20 percent. It pairs with apps and head units over both common wireless standards, and it has a built-in handle for moving it between rooms.

If your trainer and power meter disagree by more than a few percent, run a calibration from the app after warming up for ten minutes.

Specifications

Max resistance 2,200 W. Climb simulation up to 20 percent. Accuracy plus or minus 1.5 percent. Weight 19 kg. Noise 57 dB at 250 W. Fits 130, 135, 142 and 148 mm. Cassette sold separately. Price $849.

Frequently asked questions

Is a direct drive trainer better than a wheel-on trainer?

For regular training, yes. It is quieter, more accurate and does not wear out your tire. A wheel-on trainer is a good cheap option if you ride indoors only occasionally.

Does the Drive DD come with a cassette?

No. Fit an 8 to 12-speed cassette to match your bike.

How loud is the Drive DD?

We measured 57 dB at 250 W, about as loud as a normal conversation. Most of the noise you hear will be your drivetrain.

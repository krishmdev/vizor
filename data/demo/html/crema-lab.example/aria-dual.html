Crema Lab Aria Dual

The Aria Dual adds a dedicated steam boiler to the Aria platform, so you can brew and steam at the same time.

Two boilers, two PIDs

A 0.6 litre brew boiler and a 1.4 litre steam boiler are each controlled by their own PID. The brew side holds temperature within about half a degree, and the steam side delivers enough pressure for back-to-back milk drinks.

A rotary pump runs quietly and can be plumbed into a water line. The eco timer switches the machine on before your morning coffee so it is fully heated when you are ready.

Who it is for

The Aria Dual suits households that make several milk drinks each morning, or anyone who wants consistent temperature when entertaining. If you mostly drink straight espresso, the single boiler Aria gives you the same brew quality for less.

Specifications

0.6 litre brew boiler, 1.4 litre steam boiler, rotary pump, 58 mm group, 2.8 litre tank, plumb-in kit included. Heat-up about 14 minutes. Price $1,299.

Frequently asked questions

Can I brew and steam at the same time on the Aria Dual?

Yes. The Aria Dual has separate brew and steam boilers, so you can pull a shot while steaming milk.

How long does the Aria Dual take to heat up?

About 14 minutes from cold for full thermal stability. Eco mode can switch it on automatically before you wake up.

What warranty does the Aria Dual have?

The Aria Dual has a two-year warranty, extendable to four years when you register the machine.

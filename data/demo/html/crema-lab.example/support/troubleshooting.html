Espresso Troubleshooting: Sour, Bitter, Fast Shots, Leaks

Most espresso problems come from grind, dose or puck preparation, not the machine. Start with the questions below.

Before you call support

Write down your dose, yield and shot time. Most of our support conversations start with these three numbers, and most problems are solved by changing one of them.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my espresso sour?

Sour espresso is usually under-extracted. Grind finer, increase the brew temperature by one or two degrees, or pull a slightly longer ratio.

Why is my espresso bitter?

Bitterness usually means over-extraction. Grind coarser, lower the temperature slightly, or stop the shot earlier. Very dark roasts can taste bitter even when dialed in.

Why is my shot running too fast?

A fast shot means the grind is too coarse or the dose is too low. Grind finer or add half a gram, and check that the basket is not overfilled or underfilled.

Why is my machine not reaching pressure?

Check that the grind is fine enough first. If a blind basket also shows low pressure, the pump or over-pressure valve may need service, or scale may be restricting flow.

How do I fix channeling?

Break up clumps with a distribution tool, tamp level, and use pre-infusion if your machine has it. Uneven puck preparation is the most common cause.

Why is water leaking from the group head?

A leak around the portafilter usually means the group gasket is worn or the portafilter is not locked fully. Gaskets harden with heat and should be replaced every 12 to 18 months.

Why won't my milk froth properly?

Use cold milk straight from the fridge, keep the steam tip just below the surface at first to stretch the milk, then lower it to create a whirlpool. Purge the wand before steaming to remove condensed water.

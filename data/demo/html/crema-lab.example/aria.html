Crema Lab Aria

The Aria is a single boiler espresso machine with PID temperature control and a commercial-size 58 mm group.

Why the Aria

The Aria gives you the controls that matter for good espresso without the size or price of a dual boiler. The PID holds brew temperature within a degree, the gauge shows you what the pump is doing, and a three-second pre-infusion wets the puck before full pressure to reduce channeling.

The steam wand has a two-hole tip that is easier to control for beginners learning latte art.

Specifications

Single 400 ml boiler with PID control. Vibration pump with nine bar over-pressure valve. 58 mm portafilter. 2 litre water tank. Ready in about five minutes. Price $499 with a two-year warranty.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Aria good for beginners?

Yes. The Aria has a PID controller, a pressure gauge and a pre-infusion mode, which makes it easy to learn on. Most owners pull drinkable shots in their first week.

How long does the Aria take to heat up?

The Aria is ready to brew about five minutes after switching on. We recommend waiting ten minutes for the portafilter to warm fully.

Can the Aria steam milk?

Yes. Flip the steam switch after brewing and the boiler reaches steam temperature in about 50 seconds. It is suited to one or two milk drinks at a time.

Descaling

Scale builds up inside boilers and pipes when hard water is heated. Descaling removes it before it blocks valves or damages heating elements.

How often to descale

It depends on your water. Test the hardness with a strip. Above 120 ppm, descale every two months. Between 60 and 120 ppm, every three to four months. Below 60 ppm, every four to six months is enough. Using filtered or blended water reduces how often you need to do it.

What to use

Use a citric acid descaler at about a 3 percent solution. Do not use vinegar. Vinegar is slow, leaves a smell, and the acetic acid is hard on brass fittings and gaskets. Never use a descaler in the steam boiler of a dual boiler machine unless the manual for your model says it is safe.

Step by step

Empty the tank and fill it with the descaling solution. Run half of it through the group head and the hot water tap. Switch the machine off and let it sit for 20 minutes. Run the rest through, then rinse with two full tanks of fresh water. On the Duo, descale the brew boiler this way and leave the steam boiler to a service visit every two years.

If the machine is slow to heat up or the pump sounds strained, scale may already be restricting flow. Descale first, and contact support if the problem continues.

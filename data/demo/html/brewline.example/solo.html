Brewline

Solo

The Brewline Solo is a compact single boiler espresso machine with a PID controller, made for people starting out with espresso at home.

What you get

The Solo uses one 350 ml stainless boiler for both brewing and steaming. A PID controller keeps the brew temperature steady, which is the single biggest upgrade over the thermostat machines most people start with. It uses the same 58 mm portafilter as our larger machines, so accessories carry over when you upgrade.

The vibration pump is fitted with an over-pressure valve set to nine bar at the factory. That keeps shots from being pushed too hard, which is a common cause of harsh, bitter espresso on budget machines.

Milk drinks on a single boiler

Because there is one boiler, you brew first and then flip the steam switch. The boiler takes about 45 seconds to climb to steam temperature. For one or two cappuccinos a day this is a small wait. If you make several milk drinks back to back, a dual boiler machine like the Duo is the better fit.

Everyday use

The drip tray is deep enough for a full morning of shots, and the 1.8 litre tank fills from the top. The Solo fits under most kitchen cabinets at 33 cm tall.

It is ready to brew about four minutes after you switch it on. The Solo costs $449 and carries a two-year warranty.

Brewline Duo

The Brewline Duo is our dual boiler espresso machine for home baristas who want to pull a shot and steam milk at the same time.

Built around two boilers

Most home machines use one boiler for both brewing and steaming, which means waiting for the temperature to swing up before you can froth milk. The Duo separates the two jobs. A small brew boiler feeds the group head, and a larger steam boiler runs the wand, so a flat white is one continuous motion instead of two.

Each boiler has its own PID controller. You set the brew temperature in half-degree steps from the front panel, and the steam boiler holds its own pressure independently. The group is a saturated 58 mm design, so it accepts standard baskets, tampers and distribution tools.

Brewing

The rotary pump is quieter than a vibration pump and gives the same pressure from the first shot to the tenth. A needle valve on the side lets you ramp pressure for a gentle pre-infusion, or leave it fully open for a classic nine bar profile. The shot timer starts automatically when you lift the lever.

If you are new to espresso, start with 18 grams in and about 36 grams out, then adjust the grind until the shot takes between 25 and 32 seconds. The Duo is forgiving here because the temperature does not drift while you experiment.

Steaming

The four-hole steam tip produces dry steam within seconds of opening the valve. Texturing 200 ml of milk for a latte takes roughly 25 seconds. The wand is insulated, so you can wipe it with a cloth right after use without burning your fingers.

Maintenance

The water tank holds 2.5 litres and slides out from the front. Descale the brew boiler every few months depending on your water, and backflush the group weekly with a blind basket. Both boilers can be drained through a service port on the underside.

Specifications

In our internal tests the brew boiler stayed within 0.4 °C of the set point across a 20-shot session. The machine reaches brewing temperature in 11 minutes from cold, and the steam boiler holds 1.2 bar. Peak power draw is 1,650 W. The brew boiler holds 0.8 litres and the steam boiler holds 1.5 litres.

“We designed the Duo for people who make two milk drinks every morning and don't want to wait between them,” said Brewline product lead Maya Ortiz.

The Brewline Duo costs $1,199 and comes with a three-year warranty on the boilers, pump and electronics.

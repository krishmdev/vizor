Mill 64

The Brewline Mill 64 is a single-dose flat burr grinder built for espresso and pour-over.

Burrs and adjustment

It uses 64 mm stainless flat burrs turning at a low speed, which keeps heat down and produces an even particle size. Adjustment is stepless, so you can make the very small changes espresso needs. The dial is marked so you can return to a pour-over setting and back without guessing.

Single dosing

You weigh your beans, pour them in, and grind the whole dose. A bellows on top pushes out the last grounds. We measured average retention below 0.2 grams, which means the coffee in your basket is the coffee you just weighed. Low retention matters most when you switch beans often or dial in a new bag.

An anti-static plate at the chute reduces clumping and mess, so the grounds fall straight into the dosing cup instead of sticking to the walls.

Details

The Mill 64 grinds about 1.8 grams per second at espresso settings. It costs $329 and comes with a two-year warranty. The burrs are rated for roughly 500 kg of coffee before replacement.

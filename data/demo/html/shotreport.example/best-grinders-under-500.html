Best Espresso Grinders Under $500, Measured

We tested six grinders under $500 for retention, speed, clumping and how finely they adjust for espresso.

Lowest retention: Brewline Mill 64

The Mill 64 retained an average of 0.18 grams across 50 doses, the lowest in the test. Its 64 mm flat burrs ground 1.8 grams per second, and the anti-static plate kept clumping low. At $329 it was our top pick for single dosing.

Best conical: Crema Lab Nano

The Nano uses 48 mm conical burrs and costs $279. Retention averaged 0.6 grams. Conical burrs produced a slightly heavier-bodied shot in blind tasting, while flat burrs gave more clarity.

Flat vs conical burrs

In our tasting, flat burrs produced clearer, brighter espresso and conical burrs produced more body. Both can make excellent espresso. Retention and adjustment matter more at this price than burr geometry.

Also tested

The Kettleworks KG2 ($199) clumped heavily and retained 1.9 grams. Two stepped grinders could not adjust finely enough for light roasts.

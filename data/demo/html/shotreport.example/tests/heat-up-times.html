How Long Do Espresso Machines Take to Heat Up? We Timed Eight

We timed how long each machine took from a cold start to a stable brew temperature at the group, not just a ready light.

Results

Brewline Solo: 4 minutes. Crema Lab Aria: 5 minutes. Kettleworks K3: 6 minutes. Brewline Duo: 11 minutes. Crema Lab Aria Dual: 14 minutes. Marlow Home: 22 minutes. Two budget thermoblock machines were ready in under 2 minutes but never reached a stable temperature.

Why it varies

Machines with more metal in the group and larger boilers take longer to heat but hold temperature better once they get there. Saturated groups and heavy E61-style groups are the slowest. Ready lights usually come on before the group is stable, so we timed with a thermocouple in the basket.

What to do about it

If your machine takes a long time to heat up, use a smart plug or the built-in timer. If a machine that used to heat quickly has slowed down, scale in the boiler is a common cause.

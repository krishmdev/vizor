Brewline Duo Review: The Most Stable Dual Boiler We've Tested

The Brewline Duo is a $1,199 dual boiler machine with separate PIDs and a rotary pump. We used it daily for three weeks.

Temperature stability

Across 30 consecutive shots, brew water at the group stayed within 0.4 °C of the set point. That was the best result in our 2026 roundup, and it showed in the cup: once dialed in, shots tasted the same from the first to the last of a busy morning.

Steam

Steaming 300 ml of cold milk took 31 seconds with the four-hole tip. The steam was dry from the first second. Beginners may find four holes fast to control, but it is excellent for back-to-back lattes.

Downsides

The pump measured 58 dB at one metre, louder than we expected from a rotary pump. Heat-up took 11 minutes, so you will want a smart plug. The drip tray is shallow for a machine this size.

“It's the machine I would buy for a house full of latte drinkers, as long as nobody is still asleep,” said our reviewer Dan Kessler.

Verdict

The Duo is the most temperature-stable machine we have measured, with strong steam, at $100 less than its closest rival. The noise and heat-up time keep it from a perfect score.

Crema Lab Aria Review: The Easiest Espresso Machine to Learn On

The Crema Lab Aria is a $499 single boiler machine with PID, a pressure gauge and pre-infusion.

Learning curve

Our two testers who had never made espresso were pulling balanced shots on day three. The pressure gauge makes it obvious when the grind is off, and pre-infusion forgave uneven tamping more than any machine at this price.

Measurements

Heat-up took 5 minutes. Brew temperature held within 1.1 °C over 20 shots. Switching to steam took 50 seconds, and texturing 200 ml of milk took 38 seconds.

Verdict

The Aria is our pick for beginners. If you make lots of milk drinks, look at a dual boiler instead.

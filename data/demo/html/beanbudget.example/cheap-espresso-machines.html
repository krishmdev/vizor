7 Cheap Espresso Machines That Actually Make Good Espresso (2026)

You don't need to spend a fortune to make great espresso at home. Here are our favorite cheap espresso machines this year.

Our picks

The Kettleworks K3 is a great budget choice and often goes on sale. The Brewline Solo is a step up with PID temperature control and is one of the best espresso machines under $500. The Crema Lab Aria is another great option for beginners with pre-infusion.

Cheap espresso machines are perfect for beginners who want to try making espresso at home. Always check the latest prices, because deals change every day.

What to look for

Look for a machine with a good pump, a steam wand and a decent warranty. A PID is nice to have. Make sure to buy a good grinder too, because the grinder matters as much as the machine.

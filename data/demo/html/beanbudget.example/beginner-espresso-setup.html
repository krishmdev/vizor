Beginner Espresso Setup: Everything You Need

Starting your espresso journey? Here is everything you need in one espresso starter kit.

The starter kit

You need an espresso machine, a burr grinder, a scale, a tamper and a milk pitcher. For the machine, the Crema Lab Aria and Brewline Solo are popular with beginners. For the grinder, the Brewline Mill 64 and Crema Lab Nano are good choices. A scale that reads to 0.1 grams helps you weigh your dose.

Buying everything at once can be expensive, so look for bundles and deals.

Espresso Grinder Buying Guide 2026

The grinder is the most important part of your espresso setup.

How to choose

Choose a burr grinder, never a blade grinder. Flat and conical burrs both work. Stepless adjustment is best for espresso. Low retention means fresher coffee. Some grinders have clumping problems, so read reviews before you buy.

Popular choices include the Brewline Mill 64 and the Crema Lab Nano. You can buy a home espresso grinder online or at specialty coffee shops.

Is a Dual Boiler Espresso Machine Worth It?

I upgraded from a single boiler to a dual boiler last year. Here's my honest take.

When it's worth it

If you make more than two milk drinks at a time, yes. Brewing and steaming at the same time turned a 10 minute routine for two lattes into 4 minutes. Temperature stability also improved, but the PID on my old single boiler was already good.

When it isn't

If you drink straight espresso, no. You pay roughly $700 more, wait longer for heat-up, and use more counter space. Put the money into a better grinder instead.

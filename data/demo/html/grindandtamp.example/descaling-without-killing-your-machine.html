Descaling Without Killing Your Espresso Machine

I've seen more machines damaged by bad descaling than by scale. This is what I do now.

How often

Test your water. My tap water is 150 ppm, so I descale every two months. A friend with 40 ppm water descales twice a year. Using bottled water with lower hardness cuts descaling way down.

What I use

A citric acid descaler, mixed weaker than the packet says. Never vinegar, it eats gaskets. On dual boiler machines I only descale the brew side myself and leave the steam boiler for a service tech, because a stuck valve on the steam side is an expensive repair.

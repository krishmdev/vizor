Why Is My Espresso Sour? A Hobbyist's Checklist

I've made a lot of sour espresso. Here's the checklist I run through now, in order.

Grind finer first

Nine times out of ten a sour shot is a fast shot. If your double is finishing in under 22 seconds, grind finer before touching anything else. Go in small steps.

Then check ratio and temperature

If the time is right and it is still sour, pull a longer ratio, say 1:2.5 instead of 1:2. Light roasts need more extraction, so raise the temperature a degree or two if your machine allows it.

Puck prep and beans

Channeling makes a shot taste sour and bitter at the same time. Use a needle tool and tamp level. Beans roasted less than four days ago can also taste sour and gassy, so let them rest.

When my shots suddenly went sour after months of good results, the cause was a worn grinder setting that had drifted coarser.
